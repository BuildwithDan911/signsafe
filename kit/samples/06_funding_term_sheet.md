# "Growth Partnership" Funding Term Sheet
**Company:** Mama Ade Foods Ltd (fictional) · **Investor:** Apex Bridge Capital Partners (fictional)

**Headline:** ₦20,000,000 growth funding for 10% ownership. Founder stays in charge!

1. **Instrument.** ₦12,000,000 paid as Series Seed Preferred Shares, and ₦8,000,000 as a 12-month Bridge Note at 4% monthly interest.
2. **Conversion.** If any Bridge Note payment is more than 7 days late, the full outstanding balance converts into Ordinary Shares equal to 41% of the Company's issued share capital.
3. **Liquidation preference.** On any sale, merger or winding-up, Preferred holders receive 3x their original purchase price, then share pro-rata in remaining proceeds with Ordinary holders.
4. **Anti-dilution.** If the Company issues shares at a lower price, the Preferred conversion price adjusts to that lowest price.
5. **Board.** The board shall have three directors: one appointed by Founder and two appointed by Investor. The Chair, appointed by Investor, has a casting vote.
6. **Consent matters.** Without Investor's prior written consent, the Company shall not hire or dismiss staff, spend over ₦300,000, change prices, open new outlets or raise further funds.
7. **Intellectual property.** The "Mama Ade" brand, recipes and trademarks are assigned to an Investor-controlled holding entity and licensed back to the Company.
8. **Drag-along.** If Investor approves a sale of the Company, all shareholders must sell on the same terms.
9. **Guarantee.** Founder personally and unconditionally guarantees all Company obligations under the Bridge Note.
10. **Timing.** This offer is valid only until 6 pm today. Our fund closes this week and the allocation goes to the next business on our list.
