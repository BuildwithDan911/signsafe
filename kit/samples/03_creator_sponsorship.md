# Influencer Sponsorship Agreement
**Between:** GlowNova Cosmetics Ltd ("Brand") and Tolu Adeyemi-Creative ("Creator"), both fictional

1. **Campaign.** Creator will publish 3 Instagram Reels and 2 TikTok videos featuring the GlowNova Dew Serum between 1 and 31 December 2026.
2. **Fee.** Brand will pay Creator ₦450,000 within 30 days of the final post going live.
3. **Approval.** Brand may request up to two rounds of reasonable edits before posting.
4. **Usage.** Brand may reshare the Content on its organic social channels during the Campaign.
5. **Definitions.** "Campaign Period" means the period beginning on the first posting date and ending sixty (60) months thereafter. "Content" includes all footage, outtakes, voice and likeness of Creator captured in connection with this Agreement. During the Campaign Period, Brand holds an exclusive, irrevocable, worldwide, sublicensable licence to edit, adapt and use the Content and Creator's name and likeness in any media, including paid advertising, packaging and broadcast, without further payment.
6. **Exclusivity.** During the Campaign Period, Creator shall not promote any beauty, personal care or wellness product.
7. **Content restrictions.** For 24 months after signing, Creator shall not publish any content reviewing, comparing or discussing skincare products, nor make any statement about Brand that Brand considers unfavourable.
8. **Termination.** If Creator breaches clauses 6 or 7, Creator shall refund all fees received plus 50% as liquidated damages.
9. **Governing law.** Laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
