# Shared Apartment Tenancy Agreement, Version 1 (sent 20 Oct 2026)
**Landlord:** Bright Homes Property Ltd (fictional) · **Tenant:** Chidi Okafor (fictional)

1. **Rent.** ₦1,200,000 per year, payable yearly in advance.
2. **Term.** 12 months from 1 November 2026.
3. **Renewal.** The tenancy ends at the end of the Term unless both parties agree in writing to renew.
4. **Rent changes.** Rent is fixed for the Term. Any increase on renewal requires 3 months' written notice.
5. **Deposit.** A caution deposit of ₦150,000 is refundable within 14 days of move-out, less documented repair costs.
6. **Repairs.** The Landlord is responsible for structural repairs, plumbing and electrical faults.
7. **Notice to leave.** Either party may end the tenancy with 1 month's written notice.
8. **Disputes.** Disputes go to the Lagos State Rent Tribunal or a court of competent jurisdiction.
