# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT FOR INDEPENDENT ENGINEERING SERVICES

**Document Reference:** MSA-2026-ENG-0842  
**Effective Date:** 10 November 2026  
**Governing Jurisdiction:** State of Delaware, United States  

---

### PARTIES

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of the Effective Date written above, by and between:

1. **Aetheris Cloud Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation having its principal place of business at 440 Montgomery Street, Suite 1800, San Francisco, CA 94104 ("Company" or "Client"); and
2. **Kaelen Vance**, operating as an independent software consultant and technical architect, residing at 128 Northfield Avenue, London, W13 9RT, United Kingdom ("Contractor").

The Company and Contractor may collectively be referred to as the "Parties," or individually as a "Party."

---

### RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, Company is an enterprise technology provider specializing in distributed cloud infrastructure, API security, and real-time observability software; and

**WHEREAS**, Contractor possesses specialized technical expertise in full-stack cloud engineering, container orchestration, distributed microservices, and secure API architecture; and

**WHEREAS**, Company desires to retain Contractor to provide professional technical consulting and software engineering services as an independent contractor, and Contractor agrees to perform such services on the terms and conditions set forth herein.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, and agreements herein contained, and for other good and valuable consideration, the sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

---

### 1. SCOPE OF SERVICES & DELIVERABLES

1.1 **Services.** Contractor shall provide senior technical architecture, backend infrastructure modernization, and API gateway integration services as outlined in Statement of Work #1 ("SOW-01"), attached hereto as Exhibit A.

1.2 **Standard of Performance.** Contractor shall perform the Services with the highest level of professional care, skill, diligence, and expertise customary in the software engineering industry. Contractor warrants that all source code delivered will be fully functional, well-documented, and compliant with modern security standards (OWASP Top 10).

1.3 **Independent Contractor Status.** The Parties expressly acknowledge and agree that Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent, partner, or joint venturer of Company. Contractor shall retain sole discretion over the exact method, manner, and hours of performing the Services, subject to agreed milestone delivery schedules.

---

### 2. COMPENSATION & PAYMENT TERMS

2.1 **Project Fee.** In consideration for the timely and satisfactory completion of the Services described in SOW-01, Company shall pay Contractor a total fixed-price engagement fee of **$18,500.00 USD**, payable in installments linked to agreed milestones:
- **Milestone 1 (Architecture Blueprint & Data Schema):** $4,500.00 USD
- **Milestone 2 (Core API Implementation & Container Pipeline):** $7,000.00 USD
- **Milestone 3 (Final Security Audit, Integration & Production Handover):** $7,000.00 USD

2.2 **Invoicing and Payment Schedule.** Upon completion and written acceptance of each Milestone by Company, Contractor shall submit a detailed itemized invoice. Company shall disburse payment for approved invoices within ninety (90) calendar days following the completion of Company's internal quality assurance inspection cycle ("Net-90 Payment Terms").

2.3 **Expenses.** Contractor shall be solely responsible for all operational expenses incurred in the performance of the Services, including hardware, software licenses, internet connectivity, and travel, unless expressly pre-authorized in writing by an executive officer of Company.

---

### 3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & PROPRIETARY RIGHTS

3.1 **Work for Hire & Assignment.** Contractor agrees that all software, code, algorithms, scripts, documentation, system architectures, workflows, and inventions conceived, developed, or reduced to practice by Contractor (solely or jointly with others) in connection with this Agreement ("Work Product") shall constitute a "work made for hire" under applicable copyright laws.

3.2 **Pre-Existing IP & Universal Assignment.** To the extent any Work Product does not qualify as a work made for hire, Contractor hereby irrevocably assigns, transfers, and conveys to Company, without additional royalty or reservation, all worldwide right, title, and interest in and to such Work Product. Furthermore, Contractor agrees that any software libraries, tools, frameworks, utility scripts, or background components utilized or incorporated into the Work Product, whether developed prior to or outside the scope of this Agreement ("Background Materials"), shall automatically become the exclusive proprietary property of Company.

3.3 **Publicity & Portfolio Restrictions.** Contractor shall not disclose, reference, or showcase any aspect of the Services, architecture diagrams, code snippets, or Work Product in Contractor's professional portfolio, case studies, website, or resume, without the prior written consent of Company's General Counsel.

---

### 4. CONFIDENTIALITY & DATA PROTECTION

4.1 **Confidential Information.** Contractor agrees to maintain the strict confidentiality of all proprietary technical, financial, and strategic information disclosed by Company. Contractor shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party and shall use it solely for the purpose of fulfilling obligations under this Agreement.

4.2 **Survival.** The confidentiality obligations contained in this Section 4 shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years, except for trade secrets, which shall remain confidential in perpetuity.

---

### 5. NON-COMPETITION & NON-SOLICITATION

5.1 **Restrictive Covenant.** In consideration of Contractor's access to Company's confidential systems, during the term of this Agreement and for a period of twenty-four (24) months following the termination of this Agreement for any reason, Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly, perform software development, architectural consulting, or advisory services for any business, organization, or entity worldwide that develops, markets, or sells cloud infrastructure, developer tools, or API management products.

5.2 **Non-Solicitation of Personnel.** Contractor shall not, for a period of twelve (12) months following termination, solicit, recruit, or entice away any employee, contractor, or vendor of Company.

---

### 6. INDEMNIFICATION & LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

6.1 **Contractor Indemnity.** Contractor agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Company, its affiliates, directors, officers, and employees from and against any and all claims, liabilities, damages, losses, costs, and expenses (including full legal fees and settlement costs) arising out of or related to: (a) any breach of Contractor's representations or warranties; (b) any allegation that the Work Product infringes or misappropriates any third-party patent, copyright, or trade secret; or (c) any operational disruption, service interruption, or data loss attributable to Contractor's code.

6.2 **Uncapped Contractor Liability.** Contractor's indemnification obligations and liability for damages under this Agreement shall be uncapped and without financial limitation, and shall encompass direct, indirect, special, incidental, and consequential damages, including loss of business profits or goodwill.

6.3 **Company Liability Cap.** Company's total cumulative liability to Contractor for any and all claims arising out of or related to this Agreement, regardless of the theory of liability (whether in contract, tort, or warranty), shall be strictly capped at the total amount actually paid by Company to Contractor in the thirty (30) days preceding the claim, or $500.00 USD, whichever is less.

---

### 7. TERM & TERMINATION

7.1 **Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until completion of the Services, unless terminated earlier pursuant to this Section 7.

7.2 **Termination for Convenience.** Company may terminate this Agreement or any pending Statement of Work at any time, with or without cause, upon three (3) calendar days' written notice to Contractor. In the event of such termination for convenience, Company shall have no obligation to disburse payment for any uncompleted or partially delivered milestones.

7.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to cure such breach within fourteen (14) calendar days of receiving written notice.

---

### 8. GENERAL PROVISIONS

8.1 **Governing Law & Dispute Resolution.** This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the State of Delaware, United States, without regard to conflict of law principles. Any dispute arising under or relating to this Agreement shall be resolved exclusively through binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association (AAA) in Wilmington, Delaware, with each party bearing its own expenses.

8.2 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior proposals, negotiations, discussions, and understandings, whether written or oral.
