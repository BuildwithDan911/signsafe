# Shared Apartment Tenancy Agreement, Version 2 (sent 2 Nov 2026)
**Landlord:** Bright Homes Property Ltd (fictional) · **Tenant:** Chidi Okafor (fictional)

1. **Rent.** ₦1,200,000 per year, payable yearly in advance.
2. **Term.** 12 months from 1 November 2026.
3. **Renewal.** The tenancy renews automatically for further 12-month terms, and the next year's rent is due on renewal unless the Tenant gives notice at least 90 days before the end of the Term.
4. **Rent changes.** The Landlord may review the rent at any time on 14 days' notice.
5. **Deposit.** A caution deposit of ₦150,000, of which ₦100,000 is a non-refundable administrative fee.
6. **Repairs.** The Landlord is responsible for structural repairs, plumbing and electrical faults.
7. **Notice to leave.** Either party may end the tenancy with 1 month's written notice.
8. **Disputes.** All disputes shall be resolved by binding arbitration before an arbitrator nominated by the Landlord, and the Tenant waives the right to go to court.
