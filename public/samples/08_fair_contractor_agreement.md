# Independent Contractor Consulting Agreement

**Client:** BluePeak Software Solutions Inc., 100 Innovation Parkway, Austin, TX 78701 (fictional)
**Contractor:** Daniel Bassey, Independent Software Consultant (fictional)
**Effective Date:** December 1, 2026

1. **Services & Scope.** Contractor will deliver full-stack cloud engineering and API performance optimization as mutually agreed in Statement of Work #1.
2. **Compensation & Rates.** Client agrees to pay Contractor a fixed rate of $85.00 USD per hour, invoiced bi-weekly. Client shall pay all undisputed invoices within fifteen (15) calendar days of receipt.
3. **Intellectual Property.** Upon receipt of full payment from Client, Contractor assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in and to custom code and deliverables created specifically for Client under this Agreement. Contractor retains all rights in pre-existing developer tools, libraries, and background frameworks.
4. **Term & Termination.** Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon providing fourteen (14) days' advance written notice. In the event of termination, Client shall promptly pay Contractor for all authorized billable hours completed up to the date of termination.
5. **Confidentiality.** Both parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of each other's proprietary technical and business information during the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months thereafter.
6. **Limitation of Liability.** Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. Each party's maximum aggregate liability arising under this Agreement shall be limited to the total fees paid by Client to Contractor in the three (3) months preceding any claim.
7. **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware.

Signed for the Client: Sarah Jenkins, VP of Engineering
Signed for the Contractor: Daniel Bassey, Independent Consultant
